Home / Screw class guide

Which screw class do I need?

The class is the screw's corrosion protection. The further you are from the sea and from heavy industry, the lower the class you need. Every ZAP screw has its class stamped on the head, from Z to Z5.

Class 1 to Class 5

Read the head marking.

Z
Class 1General indoor use
Heat-treated carbon steel
Dry, indoor environments where corrosion exposure is minimal.
Z2
Class 2Light outdoor or covered
Heat-treated carbon steel
Light outdoor or sheltered areas with limited moisture.
Z3
Class 3Moderate outdoor
Mechanically galvanised carbon steel
Outdoor structures and moderate weather exposure.
Z4
Class 4Heavy-duty outdoor
Mechanically galvanised carbon steel
Harsh outdoor or industrial environments needing high corrosion resistance.
Z5
Class 5Marine and coastal
304 stainless steel with zinc clear coating. Bi-metal option with a carbon steel drill point.
Coastal, marine or high-moisture sites that need maximum corrosion protection.

ZAP warranty

Warranty years by environment.

Kare warrants ZAP self-drilling screws with Z3, Z4 and Z5 head markings against loss of structural integrity from corrosion or metallurgical defects.

EnvironmentZAP 3ZAP 4ZAP 5
ISO Category 2

Offshore and up to 100 m from breaking surf. Heavy industrial fallout, sulphur and acid.		Not recommendedNot recommended20 yrs

Wash unexposed areas. Soffits, cladding under eaves, garage doors and the underside of gutters don't get rinsed by rain. Kare recommends washing them at least every six months, more often at the coast or near heavy industry.

Conditions. Claims are made in writing and inspected by Kare, whose environmental classification is final. The warranty covers the structural performance of the screws from installation, not the look of the coating.

About Kare Industrial Suppliers

Kare Industrial Suppliers (Pty) Ltd supplies roofing fasteners, screws, Dektite pipe flashings and roofing hardware to contractors, builders and distributors across South Africa.

Why Kare

A complete roof fixing solution.

We specialise in the roofing and cladding industry. That focus means the person you speak to knows the difference between a T17 timber point and a No.3 drill point, and which one your purlins need.

Every product is chosen to last, and our ZAP screws carry a written warranty matched to the environment they're installed in. We build long-term relationships with our customers, with guidance and support that make every project easier.
